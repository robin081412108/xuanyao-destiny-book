# Visual Direction

## Palette

- Obsidian black
- Dark gold
- Warm parchment text
- Low-opacity orbit lines

## Motifs

- Celestial orbit
- Starfield
- Geometric seal
- Premium BaZi and Four Pillars archive

## Avoid

- Cheap red-yellow visual treatment
- Dense talisman stacking
- Human master portraits
- Panic-based imagery
